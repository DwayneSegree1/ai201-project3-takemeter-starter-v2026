analysis: states a conclusion backed by a specific, checkable fact (a stat, an age, an injury, a contract or rule detail, or a named comparison), where the fact is the reason for the conclusion rather than a restatement of it, and is broad enough to support it
hot_take: a confident claim about players, teams, or the league that would still be a claim after tonight's game is over, with no checkable support offered
reaction: the writer's immediate response to something happening right now (a play, tonight's game, or the broadcast), describing what they're seeing or feeling rather than claiming anything beyond tonight
